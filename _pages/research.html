Physics-Constrained Adaptive State Estimation

Physics constrained learning of stochastic characteristics — IFAC-PapersOnLine, 2025.

This research investigates learning-based prediction of process and measurement noise covariances for adaptive Kalman filtering. The approach combines measurement and innovation histories with statistical constraints based on innovation autocorrelation and normalized innovation squared to assess noise characteristics. Applied to vehicle sideslip angle and yaw-rate estimation, the study compares training objectives and examines their influence on estimation accuracy under model uncertainty.

Online Identification of Skidding Modes

Online identification of skidding modes with interactive multiple model estimation — ICRA 2025.

This research develops an interactive multiple model (IMM) filtering framework to identify the operating modes of skid-steered mobile robots as wheel-ground interactions change. A bank of motion models represents predefined behaviors, and measurement updates determine the probability of each mode. Evaluated using a Husky mobile robot, the framework tracks changes associated with skidding and loss of traction, supporting motion estimation and providing information relevant to robot navigation and control.
